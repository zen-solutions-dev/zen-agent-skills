- [x] (2026-11-23) Closed [`feat-0082`](.tasks/done/feat-0082-an-agent-may-not-edit-its-own-judge.md): **`A11`, do not edit the judge.** The acceptance command, the tests it runs, and any threshold an agent's work is measured against are not the agent's to change unless the task scopes it; a check that looks wrong is a finding to report, and "done" is never reached by deleting, skipping, loosening, or special-casing what measures it. Like `A10` it applies to every run, because the property belongs to the judge rather than to the run. Cited by two closed tasks that already behaved this way (`bug-0013`, `bug-0010`) and by published measurements of models editing the tests that judge them (ImpossibleBench, arXiv:2510.20270; METR, 2025-06-05); the boundary form follows `loop-design-check` in everything-claude-code (Affaan Mustafa, MIT). In [`autonomy.md`](.agents/rules/autonomy.md), the always-loaded [`core.md`](.agents/rules/core.md), the citations record, and [`fix-batch`](.agents/skills/fix-batch/SKILL.md)'s dispatch prompt.
