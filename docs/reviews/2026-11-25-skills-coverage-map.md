## The short version

**Counts over his 39 skills on `main`:** Covered 12, Partial 12, Missing 6, Declined 9. Over his 27 promoted (stable) skills alone: Covered 11, Partial 11, Missing 2 (`prototype`, `wait-what`), Declined 3 (`triage`, `wayfinder`, `teach`).

1. **We have nearly all of the main flow.** Grilling, the glossary, tdd, retro, architecture, the router, setup, research and the orchestrator are adopted in our own words with provenance. Where we differ it is mostly by adding rigour (evidence gates, verdicts, the `A8` guards, validators).
2. **The gaps are specific techniques inside skills we already have**, not whole skills: the Fowler smell baseline in review, the "smallest visual" summary in PR bodies, the debugging techniques (`git bisect run`, perf branch, ranked hypotheses, redaction), the deep-module vocabulary as a lens other skills can load, inline glossary writes during grilling, and the orchestrator's exploration child and memory lint.
3. **Two whole skills worth having are absent and nothing declines them:** `prototype` (the 2026-11-24 delta said adopt as a draft, then the Marjana plan dropped it without a word) and `wait-what` (read by the delta, given no verdict).
4. **One rule is now in tension with our own config.** Tracking is "deliberately local" (`ROADMAP.md:103`, `:247-250`; `new-task/SKILL.md:204`), yet `docs/agents/zen.md` accepts `tracker: github|gitlab` and five `role.*` labels that `init-worktracking` asks for and prints `gh label create` commands for, and **no skill reads them**. `triage` was declined (`sources-delta.md:214`), so the configured labels have no consumer.
5. **Several adoptions the delta promised were not carried through**: the exploration child, `software-factory`'s brakes and five questions in `orchestrate`, a `claude --bg` optional line in `agent-handoff`, a `wayfinder` roadmap entry, the destructive-git gate and the Codex sidecar (both "owed", `feat-0094:93-94`), and the home-repository pattern note.
6. **His HEAD has not moved since `d653911`.** A release branch bumps him to 1.3.3, and four unmerged branches carry new work (`constrain`, `animatic`/`setup-animatic`, tracker file attachments, an npm-publish wizard).
